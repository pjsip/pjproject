PROSPECT-SCENARIO MEASUREMENTS (Scott Godin / SIP Spectrum asks)
================================================================
Question: peak heap HIGH-WATER MARK (not pool-fill %) for 4 concurrent
bidirectional calls + 1 receive-only RTP stream, G.711 AND G.722 mixed,
no video/ICE/AEC, on something closer to the target than a 2007 desktop.
Libre calibration cited by prospect: ~25 KB steady, high-30s KB peak.

Scenario "mixed5": 4 bidirectional TLS+SRTP calls (calls 1-2 G.722,
calls 3-4 G.711/PCMA via live codec-priority switch on the caller) +
a 5th call placed on hold by the caller, so the callee carries one
receive-only (decode-only) RTP stream.  Callee is the measured side.

Two independent instruments, three builds -- all agree within ~0.2%:

  scenario            instrument         PEAK hwm      STEADY (settled)
  ------------------  -----------------   -----------   ----------------
  mixed 4+1 (prospect) i386 massif        892,240 B     654,416 B
  mixed 4+1 (prospect) i386 malloc-shim   890,532 B     652,676 B *
  mixed 4+1 (prospect) ARM  malloc-shim   890,532 B     652,532 B  (qemu, ARM build)
  4 calls G.722        i386 massif        739,560 B     561,520 B
  4 calls G.722        i386 malloc-shim   738,844 B     559,964 B
  4 calls G.722        ARM  malloc-shim   738,388 B     559,964 B  (qemu, ARM build)
  1 call  G.722        i386 massif        418,576 B     302,776 B
  1 call  G.722        i386 malloc-shim   418,520 B     295,788 B
  idle (0 calls)       i386 massif        208,104 B     208,104 B
  idle (0 calls)       ARM  malloc-shim   213,076 B     204,360 B  (qemu, ARM build)
  * mixed5s shim: settled 652,676 B at t=69..107 after ~32 s transient decay

HEADLINE FOR THE PROSPECT (4 calls + 1 recvonly, mixed G.711/G.722):
  Peak malloc'd heap high-water mark   ~= 891 KB  (~870 KiB)
  Settled steady-state malloc'd heap   ~= 653 KB  (~637 KiB)
  32-bit ARM and 32-bit x86 are within 0.2% -- 32-bit pointer size, and
  heap is dominated by identically-sized buffers on both arches.

The PEAK is a setup-burst artifact: all 5 calls set up inside ~20 s, so
their SIP transaction / tx_data transients stack.  They age out ~32 s
after the last INVITE; steady is ~238 KB below peak.  Staggered call
admission keeps peak near steady.

WHAT THE BYTES BUY (mixed5 peak attribution, massif):
  pjlib pools               813,384 B (91%)   <- see breakdown below
  mbedTLS record buffers     33,530 B         (2 x 16 KB in/out, ONE shared TLS conn)
  libsrtp contexts           15,996 B         (~3.2 KB x 5 streams)
  glibc stdio                12,288 B
  x509 / bignum handshake    ~4-9 KB
pjlib pool total when all 5 calls up: 617,856 B capacity / 525,778 B used (85%).

WHY PJSIP IS ~26x LIBRE'S 25 KB, AND THE APPLES-TO-APPLES CAVEAT:
- Preallocated call array: pjsua-lib allocates all max_calls slots at
  pjsua_init: 17,592 B x 5 = 87,960 B, present at idle (before any call).
  libre allocates per call, on demand.
- Jitter buffers: pjsua default jb_max = 500 ms of frames PER STREAM,
  preallocated at stream open (~16 KB stream pool per call).
- One shared TLS connection's mbedTLS buffers = 33.5 KB (16+16 KB records).
  A prospect allocator that instrumented only libre's own mem_alloc would
  NOT see OpenSSL/mbedTLS/libsrtp heap at all -- so his 25 KB is very
  likely libre-core-only, while our 653 KB is TOTAL process malloc (every
  library included, via LD_PRELOAD).  Not the same denominator.
- pjsua-lib is a batteries-included high-level layer (conference bridge,
  presence buddy table, account mgr, media transport abstraction).

LIKE-FOR-LIKE FIGURE if excluding the TLS library + libsrtp (closer to
what a libre-core allocator captures): pjlib pools only ~ 462-529 KB used.
Still ~20x libre -- the gap is architectural (pool preallocation + JB),
not measurement noise.

SHRINK KNOBS (measured elsewhere in this report):
  PJSUA_MAX_BUDDIES 8         -154 KB static (not heap, but total RAM)
  max_calls 5 -> 4            -17.6 KB heap (one fewer preallocated slot)
  jb_max 500 -> ~120 ms       shrinks the ~16 KB/stream stream pool
  mbedTLS 4 KB TLS records    -24.6 KB peak (measured)
  Dropping pjsua-lib for raw pjmedia+pjsip would remove the 88 KB call
  array + conference bridge overhead, at the cost of writing that glue.

CODEC CPU RATIO (Scott's DSP-vs-MCU question; PJSIP's own G.722/G.711):
Microbenchmark: encode+decode of one 10 ms frame, x86 -m32 -Os, under
valgrind callgrind (instruction reads, Ir) and native wall-clock.
  codec                         Ir/frame     us/frame (native)
  G.722/16000                   219,481      28.24
  G.711 PCMU, computed (default)  9,613       1.02
  G.711 PCMA, computed (default)  9,853       1.10
  G.711 PCMU, table-based         2,096       0.35
  ratio G.722 / G.711 computed  = 22.8x (Ir), 27.7x (wall clock)
  ratio G.722 / G.711 table     = 104.7x (Ir)
INTERPRETATION: G.722 is the fixed cost (fixed-point, 24-tap QMF analysis
+ synthesis, full sub-band ADPCM, at 2x the sample rate of G.711).  The
RATIO is governed entirely by how cheap G.711 is.  The 2007 "~55x on
ARM9" figure is consistent with a TABLE-based G.711 (near-free lookups):
the ratio lands between our 23x (computed) and 105x (table).  PJSIP's
PJ_CONFIG_MINIMAL_SIZE preset sets PJMEDIA_HAS_ALAW_ULAW_TABLE 0, making
G.711 computed and the ratio ~23x -- but that is a flash/RAM-vs-CPU knob:
enabling the 2x 256-byte tables restores near-free G.711 and a ~100x ratio.
For a DSP-vs-MCU decision (order of magnitude): one bidirectional G.722
call = 1 encode + 1 decode per 10 ms frame = 219,481 Ir/frame = 21.9 MIr/s.
Four G.722 calls ~= 87.8 MIr/s.  On an in-order M-class core near 1 IPC
that is ~88 MHz-equivalent of pure codec work -- i.e. roughly a third to
a half of a 200-250 MHz Cortex-M just for the four G.722 legs, before
RTP/SRTP/jitter/mixing.  Four G.711 calls (computed) ~= 3.8 MIr/s;
negligible.  This is the gap that motivates a DSP/HW codec path: G.722 in
software is real load on an MCU; G.711 is not.

EMBEDDED-TUNED VARIANT (measured, run-tuned5): PJSUA_MAX_BUDDIES 8 +
mbedTLS 4 KB record buffers + --jb-max-size 200 ms, same mixed 4+1 scenario.
  PEAK heap   = 842,652 B (822.9 KiB)   (stock mixed5 peak 890,532)
  STEADY heap = 632,676 B (617.8 KiB)   (stock mixed5 steady 652,676)
  47 s settled plateau t=69..115; codecs confirmed 2xG722 + 2xPCMA
  bidirectional + 1 PCMA recvonly (held).
  Tuning moved heap only ~20 KB steady / ~48 KB peak: buddies is STATIC
  RAM (not heap); jb 500->200 ms trims the per-stream pool; 4 KB TLS is a
  peak-only saving.  Heap is dominated by the 88 KB preallocated call
  array + per-call INVITE/dialog/stream pools, which these knobs don't touch.

CORRECTED FIT ANALYSIS -- prospect's exact scenario, STM32H563 (655,360 B):
Static (.data+.bss) and heap (malloc) are ADDITIVE, separate memory.
The 88 KB call array is on the heap (pjsua_var.calls is a pointer);
buddy[256]=158 KB is in static .bss.  No double count.

  config    static     heap-steady   TOTAL steady   heap-peak   TOTAL peak
  --------  ---------   -----------   ------------   ---------   ----------
  stock      225,188      652,676        877,864      890,532    1,115,720
  tuned       71,428      632,676        704,104      842,652      914,080
  (+ 5 thread stacks ~40 KB @ 8 KB default, + lwIP pools = prospect's side)

vs 655,360 B (640 KB): tuned STEADY total 704 KB is ~49 KB OVER; peak
totals are ~900 KB-1.1 MB.  => At the prospect's exact scenario, PJSIP
does NOT fit 640 KB even tuned.  Realistic RAM need is ~0.9-1.1 MB.

This CORRECTS the original report's Section 5, which modelled 4 single-
codec G.722 calls (heap steady 561 KB) and concluded "fits with 22 KB
spare after buddies=8".  The prospect's 4+1 MIXED scenario has ~92 KB
more heap (the 5th stream + per-stream resamplers for the 8 kHz G.711
ports on the 16 kHz bridge), so tuned total is 704 KB, not 633 KB.

The 5th (recvonly) stream here is a SIP-signalled HELD call -- an UPPER
BOUND; a raw RTP listener (no dialog/tsx, decode+jitter+SRTP only) would
cost less.  Marginal cost of the 5th stream: stock 652,676 - 561,520
(4-call g722) is not directly comparable (mixed vs single codec); within
mixed5 the per-call steps are ~110-120 KB during setup, settling lower.

BOTTOM LINE FOR THE PART DECISION:
  - Flash: fine (~1.03 MiB of 2 MB).
  - RAM at this scenario: ~0.9-1.1 MB needed; 640 KB is short by a wide
    margin even after tuning.  Options: (a) a part with ~1 MB+ SRAM;
    (b) drop below pjsua-lib to raw pjsip+pjmedia (removes the 88 KB call
    array + conference bridge/resamplers, biggest single heap item);
    (c) aggressive jitter-buffer + fewer preallocated calls; (d) a lighter
    stack.  This is why libre's ~25 KB matters: it is a different class of
    footprint, and if 640 KB is fixed, that gap decides the part number.
