TLS connection lifecycle (measured): the single TLS connection carrying
all calls is destroyed by pjsip's idle timer ~32 s after the last INVITE
transaction ends (b.log: "destroyed due to timeout in idle timer");
media continues on SRTP. Steady figures therefore exclude a live TLS
connection on the callee. Live-connection cost (massif attribution):
  mbedtls_ssl_setup 2 x 16,765 = 33,530 B (16 KB in + 16 KB out record
  buffers) + ~6 KB transport pool. With MBEDTLS_SSL_IN/OUT_CONTENT_LEN
  = 4096: 2 x 4,477 = 8,954 B, and the 4-call PEAK drops 739,560 ->
  714,984 (-24.6 KB). Steady is unchanged (connection closed anyway).
Caller keeps its client-side connection open through the hold: caller
steady is ~88 KB above callee steady, consistent with the live TLS
context + larger UAC dialog/tsx state (caller residue 96 KB).

Peak attribution at the 4-call peak (snapshot 29):
  pjlib pools 665,080 (90%; steady pools are 528,792 -> ~136 KB of SIP
  transaction/tdata transients that age out ~32 s after setup)
  mbedtls_ssl_setup 33,530; srtp_crypto_alloc 12,664 (~3.2 KB/call);
  glibc stdio 12,288; x509/bignum ~4 KB; misc ~7.4 KB
Handshake: TLS-ECDHE-RSA-WITH-CHACHA20-POLY1305-SHA256, RSA-2048 cert.

Per-call pool growth (new pools in the 1-call dump vs base):
  inv session 31,024+1,024; strm 16,096 (incl. jbuf, jb_max=500 ms
  default = 25 x 20 ms frames); media transport 10,752; dlg 8,096;
  srtp 6,024; group locks/clock/evsub etc ~4-5 KB.

G.711 (PCMU, 8 kHz bridge) vs G.722 at 4 calls: steady 554,232 vs
561,520 (-7.3 KB) — codec choice is RAM-neutral at this scale.

Threads: 5 per pjsua instance (measured /proc/<pid>/task during run).
pjlib PJ_THREAD_DEFAULT_STACK_SIZE is 8192; on an RTOS port, task
stacks are the port's choice and are NOT in the heap numbers.

FIT MATH vs STM32H563 (640 KB = 655,360 B SRAM, 2 MB flash):
  flash: 1,084,122 B (log level 4) / 1,001,839 B (log 0) -> ~52%. FITS.
  RAM, stock config:      static 225,188 + steady 561,520 = 786,708 B
                          (+ peak 739,560 -> 964,748 B worst case). NO FIT.
  RAM, buddies=8 variant: static 71,428 + steady 561,520 = 632,948 B
                          (~22 KB margin, before stacks/RTOS/lwIP);
                          peak-based: 71,428 + 739,560 = 810,988. OVER.
  Peak mitigations measured: 4 KB TLS buffers -24.6 KB; the ~136 KB
  tsx/tdata transient decays 32 s after call setup and scales with how
  many calls are set up within a 32 s window.
